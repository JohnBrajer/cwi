# CWI

**John Brajer — behavioral corridor framework**  
Public version: 0.1 — September 25, 2026

> The acronym is intentionally left unexpanded until John Brajer defines a canonical expansion.

## Core mechanism

People may possess broad literal freedom while exercising choice inside a much narrower socially and psychologically permitted **behavioral corridor**.

An unconventional action can initially carry:

- high uncertainty,
- low legibility,
- low social proof,
- high differentiation.

Once someone visibly succeeds, the action becomes easier for others to conceive as available.

Repeated observations update the corridor:

```
unimagined -> conceivable -> attempted -> legible -> normalized
```

As adoption increases, perceived uncertainty can fall while novelty and status differentiation often fall with it.

## Key implication

The practical constraint on a person's action space may be less about the number of actions that physically exist and more about the number of actions they psychologically permit themselves to consider.

## Early / late asymmetry

Early movers can face maximum uncertainty with potentially high differentiation.

Later movers receive more evidence and social permission, but the action may provide less differentiation precisely because it is now legible.

PRS is a decision lens nested under CWI.
